      About the project

      weather_glass is an experimental project that visualizes the air's flow at a given moment as flow-field particles.
      Scroll through a city's chapter and watch the flow shift with its real-time weather.

      The time slider adjusts brightness between day and night,
      while each city's live wind, precipitation, and cloud cover set the flow's speed, direction, and turbulence.
